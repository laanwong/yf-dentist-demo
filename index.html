About Your Family Dentist

High quality care, with honest communication.

Your Family Dentist provides traditional dental care for the whole family, from young children to mature adults. Their experienced dentists and friendly staff use easy-to-understand communication to help patients feel comfortable.

The practice aims to provide valuable dental services with transparent treatment plans and no concealed fees, plus flexible appointments for preventive care and emergencies.

  • Fully accredited: awarded full accreditation against the NSQHS Standards for Dental Practice Accreditation.
  • Safety and quality: practice systems are designed around patient safety, quality and continuous improvement.
  • Your rights and privacy: patients are respected, informed and involved in care decisions, with privacy protections in place.
  • Clear information: the team supports patients with information needed to make care decisions.
